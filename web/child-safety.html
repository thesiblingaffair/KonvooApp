Child Safety Standards

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Konvoo has a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation. The safety of minors is our highest priority.

1. Our Commitment

Konvoo ("we", "us", "our") is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. We actively work to detect, report, and remove any content that endangers children. This policy applies to all users, all conversations, and all content generated through our AI systems.

2. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on Konvoo and will result in immediate action:

  • Any sexual or suggestive content involving minors (anyone under 18 years of age)
  • Content that sexualizes, objectifies, or exploits children in any form
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including AI-generated
  • Content that facilitates, encourages, or promotes grooming behavior
  • Attempts to use our AI to generate inappropriate content involving minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or soliciting exploitative material involving children
  • Content that promotes or normalizes inappropriate relationships with minors

3. Technical Safeguards

We implement multiple layers of technical protection:

  • AI System Prompt Guardrails: Our AI character (Kavya) is programmed with hard-coded rules that absolutely prohibit generating any content involving minors. These rules cannot be overridden by user input.
  • Content Moderation: All AI-generated responses pass through content safety filters that flag and block any content that could involve or endanger minors.
  • Input Monitoring: User messages are monitored for patterns associated with CSAM or grooming behavior.
  • Age Verification: Our platform requires phone number verification for account creation, which serves as a basic age gate.

4. Reporting and Response

We take the following actions when CSAE content is detected or reported:

  • Immediate removal of any offending content
  • Immediate suspension of the user's account
  • Preservation of evidence for law enforcement
  • Reporting to authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via their CyberTipline, and Indian authorities as required under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations as legally required

5. How to Report

If you encounter any content on Konvoo that you believe involves the exploitation of a child, please report it immediately through any of the following channels:

  • Email: safety@konvoo.live
  • Email: support@konvoo.live
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline
  • Childline India: 1098

All reports are treated with the highest urgency and confidentiality.

6. User Responsibility

All users of Konvoo must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age to use the platform
  • Not use the platform to create, share, or solicit any content that exploits or endangers children
  • Report any suspicious activity or content that may involve child exploitation
  • Comply with all applicable laws regarding child protection, including the POCSO Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000

7. Training and Review

Our team regularly reviews and updates our child safety measures, including:

  • Quarterly review of AI safety guardrails and content moderation systems
  • Ongoing training on CSAE identification and reporting procedures
  • Regular updates to our detection and prevention systems
  • Compliance reviews with evolving legal requirements in India and globally

8. Legal Compliance

Konvoo operates in compliance with:

  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 — India
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021 — India
  • Google Play Developer Policy on Child Safety
  • NCMEC reporting obligations
