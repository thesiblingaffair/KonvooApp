Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 March 2026

This policy applies to all paid subscriptions on the Konvoo platform operated by Brewing Conversations Private Limited. We aim to be transparent and fair with all payment-related matters.

QUICK SUMMARY

✅ Cancel anytime — no questions asked
✅ Keep access until your billing period ends
✅ ₹9 trial fee is non-refundable
✅ Full refund within 48 hours of first charge if unused
✅ Refunds processed within 5–7 business days

1. Subscription Plans

PlanPriceBilling

2. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your Pro subscription at any time through:

  • The Profile section in the Konvoo app (Profile → Settings → Cancel)
  • Contacting us at support@konvoo.live

Upon cancellation:

  • You will retain access to all Pro features until the end of your current billing period.
  • No further charges will be applied after the current period.
  • Your account will automatically revert to the Free plan once the paid period expires.
  • Your conversation history, memories, and data will be preserved on the Free plan.

3. Refund Policy

Monthly Subscription (₹99):

  • Within 48 hours of charge: If you request a refund within 48 hours of being charged and have not substantially used Pro features during that period, you are eligible for a full refund of that month's charge.
  • After 48 hours: Refunds are generally not provided for the current billing period. You may cancel to prevent future charges and continue using Pro until the period ends.
  • Technical issues: If you experience technical issues that prevent you from using the Platform and we are unable to resolve them, you may be eligible for a pro-rated refund at our discretion.
  • Unauthorized charges: If you believe a charge was unauthorized, contact us immediately. We will investigate and issue a refund if the charge is confirmed unauthorized.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email support@konvoo.live with:

  • Your registered phone number
  • Date of the charge
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Razorpay payment ID (if available, found in your payment confirmation)

6. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method (UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, or wallet).
  • Bank processing times may vary. If you don't see the refund after 7 business days, please contact your bank or payment provider.

7. Failed Payments

If a recurring payment fails:

  • Razorpay will automatically retry the charge.
  • If payment continues to fail, your subscription will be halted and access will revert to the Free plan.
  • You can resubscribe at any time by choosing a new subscription from the Profile section.
  • No penalty or additional charges will be applied for failed payments.

8. Account Deletion

If you delete your Konvoo account while having an active subscription:

  • Your subscription will be cancelled immediately.
  • No refund will be issued for the remaining period of the current billing cycle.
  • All data including conversations, memories, and payment history will be permanently deleted.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated via the Platform.
